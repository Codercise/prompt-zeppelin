Your script, right under your webcam

Prompt Zeppelin is a teleprompter for your Mac. It shows your words just below the camera, a few at a time, so you can read naturally on calls and recordings.

View on GitHub Download coming soon

How it works

  1. Write your script

    Paste in what you want to say, or type it straight into the editor.

  2. Open the teleprompter

    It appears at the top of your screen, just under the camera. Hold Shift and drag to fine-tune where it sits.

  3. Press play

    Words move along at the speed you set. Pause, stop or change the speed whenever you need to.

Prompt Zeppelin isn't ready to download just yet. Follow the project on GitHub to hear when it is, or build it yourself today.

Build it yourself

You'll need macOS 14 or later, Xcode 16 and XcodeGen.

brew install xcodegen
git clone {{ site.Params.github }}.git
cd prompt-zeppelin
xcodegen generate
open PromptZeppelin.xcodeproj

Then press Run in Xcode.
